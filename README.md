# THE STREET BOOK

Covenant Builders' field guide for storm door-knocking on the Treasure Coast and South Brevard.

Live: https://kingofthewisdomrealm-hub.github.io/STREETS/

Three storm areas, seven routes. Each route gives you:

- the neighbourhood from the air (USDA NAIP aerial photography, public domain, plus Esri and street-name layers)
- every door on the street in walking order, with the address, the owner of record and the year the house was built, pulled live from the Florida Department of Revenue 2025 cadastral
- the National Weather Service storm reports plotted where they were called in
- a blue dot showing where you are standing, and the nearest door
- the route handed off to Google Maps for driving
- the local history, the legends, the local heroes and the landmarks — with a source on every line
- what to say, what never to say, and the Florida statutes that decide which is which
- a printable walk sheet

Nothing personal is stored in this repository. Owner names are read live from the state's
public tax roll the same way the property appraiser publishes them, and what you mark on a
door stays in your own phone's browser storage.
